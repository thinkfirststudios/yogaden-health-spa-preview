About Us

Where Eastern Wisdom Meets Modern Living

Our Mission

More than a yoga studio—YogaDen is a sanctuary for mind, body, heart, and soul.

Rooted in the timeless wisdom and science of yoga, our mission is to support health, healing, and personal transformation through practices that unite body, mind, and spirit.

Inspired by the Yamas and Niyamas, we strive to create a welcoming space where people feel supported, connected, and empowered on their journey toward greater well-being.

We are committed to fostering a community where individuals can cultivate self-awareness, strengthen their bodies, calm their minds, open their hearts, and deepen their connection to something greater than themselves.

At YogaDen, we believe true wellness is about more than exercise—it's about creating a balanced, meaningful life rooted in movement, mindfulness, compassion, and connection.

Meet Leeza Villagomez

Founder • Certified Yoga Therapist (C-IAYT) • E-RYT 500 • YACEP

C-IAYT is certification by the International Association of Yoga Therapists. RYS 200, RYS 300, E-RYT 500 and YACEP are Yoga Alliance registrations. View the school profile

Her journey began with a life-changing chakra meditation that sparked a lifelong passion for yoga, healing, and self-discovery. Raised between Southern California and Maui, Leeza's early years were filled with nature, horseback riding, and Hawaiian, Tahitian, Polynesian, and Māori dance—experiences that shaped her deep appreciation for movement, culture, and community.

Her mission is simple: to empower others to move, breathe, heal, and thrive.

Our History

Our journey started by offering classes in local dance studios and community centers throughout Corona and Norco.

As interest continued to grow, so did YogaDen.

Our light-filled studio, surrounded by natural beauty, provides a peaceful sanctuary where students can reconnect with themselves and experience the transformative power of yoga.

YogaDen Health Spa & International Yoga School is a comprehensive wellness center dedicated to supporting the whole person—body, mind, heart, and spirit.

Our offerings include:

  • Yoga Classes for All Levels
  • Yoga Therapy
  • Guided Meditation
  • Breathwork
  • Sound Healing
  • Yoga Alliance Certified 200 & 300 Hour Teacher Training
  • Continuing Education Programs (CECs & CEUs)
  • Seasonal Wellness Retreats
  • International Yoga Retreats
  • Holistic Wellness Workshops
