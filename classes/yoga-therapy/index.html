Yoga Therapy

Personalized One-on-One Sessions

What to Expect During Your Yoga Therapy Session

Your journey begins with a private one-on-one consultation with Leeza Villagomez, Certified Yoga Therapist (C-IAYT).

Together, you'll develop a personalized wellness plan designed specifically for your goals and health needs.

Your session may include:

  • A personalized yoga therapy program tailored specifically for you
  • Education and self-care tools you can confidently practice at home

No prior yoga experience is necessary.

Whether you're new to yoga or have practiced for years, every session is thoughtfully adapted to meet you exactly where you are.

Movement • Breathwork • Meditation • Mindfulness

Each session is 60 minutes · In-Person & Virtual Sessions Available

C-IAYT is certification by the International Association of Yoga Therapists.

Differing Focus

Yoga Class

  • Instruction in yoga techniques
  • General practice, often fitness-oriented
  • Limited individual adaptations
  • Community practice

Yoga Therapy Session

  • Individual assessment, formal intake
  • Practices tailored to client goals
  • Therapeutic relationship, possibly in a group

Yoga Therapy is designed to complement—not replace—the care provided by your physician or other healthcare professionals.
