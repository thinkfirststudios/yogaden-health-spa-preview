Private Events, Celebrations & Corporate Experiences

Create an experience your guests will remember long after the day is over.

At YogaDen, we design beautiful, intentional private experiences for birthdays, bachelorette celebrations, bridal gatherings, friend groups, corporate teams, wellness days, retreats and life's meaningful milestones.

Whether you gather at YogaDen, at your home or venue, or at a destination,
we'll help create an experience that feels uniquely yours.

Tell us who you're celebrating, what you're celebrating and how you want everyone to feel—and we'll help dream up the rest.

Experiences can include:

  • Private Yoga, Gentle Yoga, Yin or Restorative Yoga
  • Sound Bath & Deep Relaxation
  • Guided Meditation & Breathwork
  • New Moon or Full Moon Circles
  • Intention-Setting Ceremonies
  • Women's Circles
  • Birthday & Milestone Rituals
  • Bachelorette Wellness Experiences
  • Bridal & Friendship Gatherings
  • Cacao-inspired Ceremonial Gatherings
  • Flower Mandala Experiences
  • Journaling & Reflection
  • Chair Yoga & Accessible Wellness Experiences
  • Corporate Wellness & Team Connection
  • Customized Retreat Days
  • Destination Yoga & Wellness Experiences

Corporate Yoga Retreats

No two companies are alike, which is why every retreat is completely customized to meet your organization's goals.

Retreats can be hosted at your office, at YogaDen, or at beautiful destinations throughout California and beyond.

Every retreat is suitable for beginners. No previous yoga experience is necessary.

Led by Leeza Villagomez, C-IAYT, E-RYT 500, YACEP, our experienced team combines the wisdom of traditional yoga with modern science to create experiences that are engaging, approachable, and transformative.

We offer:

  • Half-Day Retreats
  • Full-Day Retreats
  • Weekend Retreats
  • Executive Leadership Retreats
  • Corporate Wellness Getaways
