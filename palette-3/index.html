Yoga for Every Body

At YogaDen, we believe yoga is for everyone.

Our classes, teacher trainings, workshops, and wellness programs offer a comprehensive and holistic approach to health and well-being. Rooted in the Eight Limbs of Yoga, we honor both the science and the art of yoga through a variety of styles, practices, and teachings designed to meet you wherever you are on your journey.

Whether you're new to yoga or a lifelong practitioner, you'll find opportunities to grow, heal, and deepen your connection to yourself.

Meet Leeza Villagomez

Raised between Southern California and Maui, Leeza's early years were filled with nature, horseback riding, and Hawaiian, Tahitian, Polynesian, and Māori dance—experiences that shaped her deep appreciation for movement, culture, and community.

Her mission is simple: to empower others to move, breathe, heal, and thrive.
